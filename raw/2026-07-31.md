## The Most Capable Humanoid Robot Yet!

Source: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Q60VNdtyiAA
Published: 2026-07-30T16:03:52Z
Type: YouTube Short

You've got to check this out. Google just revealed Gemini Robotics 2, the software behind this. It sounds really simple, but it's way more than that. It's actually insanely difficult for a robot to coordinate all five fingers and grip things with different levels of strength. So, something like tying up a trash bag into a knot is an insane task for a robot to be able to do. Its hands have 22 different degrees of movement, so it can also seal ziplock bags, unscrew light bulbs, grab grapes without breaking them, and pack objects into tight spaces. Google's previous robot mostly works from the waist up. But Gemini Robotics 2 works from feet to fingertips. And they're even teaching multiple robots to communicate and work together on the same job. The same underlying AI can be adapted to completely different robot bodies in just a few hours. And an ondevice version can run locally without an internet connection. And this story just dropped today.
