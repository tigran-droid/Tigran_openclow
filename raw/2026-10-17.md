🎥 The core idea is different from most video tools: instead of typing 'slow dolly left' and hoping the model interprets it correctly, camera movement goes in as actual geometry, a position and angle in 3D space. From a single starting image, Atlas can output up to one minute of 1440p video that follows that exact trajectory.

🧩 The same model reconstructs real locations too. World Labs says it can rebuild a place from one or more images, generate unseen angles, and export explicit geometry as point clouds or 3D Gaussian splats, the same format used in Marble. More reference photos mean less the model has to invent.

🤖 What's new in this beta is reusability: generated views can be reused, with their camera and depth data, as context for later shots, turning single generations into an extendable world rather than disconnected outputs. World Labs has also shown the same spatial context generating robot training data, photorealistic RGB and depth exactly as a robot's sensors would see it.
