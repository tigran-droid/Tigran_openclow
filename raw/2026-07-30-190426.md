# Advancing the price-performance frontier with GPT-5.6

Source: https://openai.com/index/advancing-the-price-performance-frontier-with-gpt-5-6
Published: 2026-07-30T10:00:00Z
Type: blog/news

OpenAI says it is passing efficiency gains from GPT-5.6 to customers through lower prices for Luna and Terra and faster performance for Sol in the API.

Starting July 30, GPT-5.6 Luna costs 80% less and GPT-5.6 Terra costs 20% less. The same changes reduce how much Terra and Luna usage counts against paid ChatGPT Work and Codex subscriptions. OpenAI says Luna can use tools and complete multi-step workflows, making more high-volume AI applications economical.

OpenAI also introduced Fast mode in the API, replacing Priority Processing. For GPT-5.6 Sol, Fast mode delivers up to 2.5 times faster speeds than Standard processing at twice the price, with no change in intelligence. Existing requests tagged priority automatically use Fast mode.

Customer examples in the announcement are vendor-provided claims. Notion reported that Terra delivered quality comparable to GPT-5.5 at half the cost per task and in 60% less time in its evaluations. Blitzy said Luna increased prompt-cache reuse from 24% to 90%, handled 2.2 times more context with 8.5 times fewer output tokens, and cost 87% less than GPT-5.4 mini across thousands of production calls. Dust said Luna was 40% faster and 40% cheaper than its previous default for the same agentic tasks.

OpenAI says model selection should depend on stakes, cost of error, urgency and scale, and that the balance can change between workflow stages. It claims Luna performs comparably to models that were frontier-class a year ago at roughly six cents on the dollar per task and nearly nine times the speed. On Agents’ Last Exam, OpenAI says Luna outperforms Fable 5 at an estimated cost per task nearly 99% lower.

The company suggests using evaluations to decide where additional intelligence improves outcomes and where lower-cost processing gives the same quality. Its example coding workflow uses Sol to resolve uncertainty and define a plan, then Luna for well-specified implementation, tests and evaluation.

OpenAI attributes the efficiency gains to model improvements, inference systems, routing, production software, context management and the agent harness. It says Sol, inside a human-led process, rewrote production kernels, ran hundreds of experiments and monitored training. The kernel work reportedly reduced end-to-end model-serving cost by 20%, while experiments increased token-generation efficiency by more than 15%.

The company says lower-cost Luna and Terra make high-volume work such as document analysis, customer-interaction classification and routine implementation more economical, while Fast mode offers lower latency for consequential Sol workloads when the premium is justified.

Starting July 30, API pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $12 per million output tokens for Terra, and $0.20 per million input tokens and $1.20 per million output tokens for Luna. Sol pricing is unchanged. ChatGPT and Codex subscription prices and quota budgets are unchanged, but Terra and Luna usage consumes fewer credits. AWS pricing changes begin rolling out later on July 30.
