# How to write LinkedIn post ideas

## Voice
- Practical, confident, direct. Written for business leaders and
  executives, not developers.
- No hype words: avoid "game-changer", "revolutionary", "unlock",
  "supercharge", "in today's fast-paced world".
- Short sentences. No corporate fluff.

## Format for each LinkedIn idea
1. **Hook** — one sharp line that stops the scroll. Often a question,
   a surprising number, or a contrarian take.
2. **Body** — 3-4 sentences. Explain the news in plain terms, then say
   what it actually means for a business leader reading this.
3. **Source** — one link to the original article.

## Output
Always produce exactly 3 LinkedIn post ideas per briefing, each based on
a different underlying story. Number them 1, 2, 3.

## Example tone
"Everyone is talking about AI agents. Almost nobody has one that
actually finishes a task end-to-end without a human checking its work.
[2-3 more sentences explaining the specific news and the takeaway]
Source: [link]"
