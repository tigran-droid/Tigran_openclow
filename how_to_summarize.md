# How to summarize collected material

This controls how raw material (articles, transcripts, episodes) is turned into
short summaries. This is separate from how posts are written.

## For every item, produce:

**Title** — the original title.

**Source** — the link, and the type (blog / YouTube / podcast).

**Summary** — 3 to 5 sentences in plain English. Say what is actually new or
claimed. No hype, no filler.

**Why it matters for us** — 1 or 2 sentences, from the point of view of an
AI-transformation consulting business and its clients.

**Key facts** — bullet list of any hard numbers, company names, dates, prices
or product names mentioned. These are what make content credible.

**Quotable line** — if the source has one strong sentence worth quoting, copy
it exactly and mark it as a quote.

## Rules
- Never invent a fact. If something is unclear in the source, write "unclear".
- Keep the original meaning. Do not soften or exaggerate claims.
- For long transcripts, summarize the argument, not the small talk, sponsor
  reads or channel promotion.
- Write for a busy executive who will read this in 30 seconds.
