# Daily news — 2026-09-18

## Zapier reaches the organisational phase
Almost 100% of Zapier employees use AI day to day. The company now sees role design, reskilling and team structure as the real constraints. Its chief people officer was asked to lead the next phase because the bottleneck had become human, not technical.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2ayx_3mHPgU

## Token budgets become management policy
Some Zapier engineers are spending as much as $30,000 a month on model usage. The company is giving people visibility into spend before imposing individual budgets.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2ayx_3mHPgU

## SaaS pricing meets headless work
Zapier expects fixed per-seat software pricing to weaken as agents use products without a human interface. Usage pricing suits commodity capability; outcome pricing works where success is clearly measurable.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2ayx_3mHPgU

## Agent teams scale faster than expected
Ethan Mollick tested a central agent that created 18 research threads, then launched more agents for analysis, writing and fact-checking. The practical question is shifting to human acceptance and spending boundaries.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2100665801800093959

## Opportunity AI moves beyond faster drafts
Interactive proposals, decision simulators, exploratory product demos and custom video pipelines are presented as current uses. The point is to turn static material into something people can inspect, change and test.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XsOoumeXtm4
