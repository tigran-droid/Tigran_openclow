# Daily news — 2026-10-05

## AI needs a team inbox
Ethan Mollick called for email built for humans and autonomous agents together.
The missing pieces are permissions, shared comments and escalation to people.
This turns email automation from a personal trick into an operating-model question.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2106995773682458784

## Audit agents enter the picture
Mollick argued for personal operating systems that show what agents are doing.
He wants fine-grained security and independent agents checking other agents.
The move is from trusting outputs to designing observable, contestable work.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2106936117421601110

## Organisations become the bottleneck
Mollick said easier AI may weaken the value of individual usage tricks.
Organisational systems increasingly constrain the gains.
Training without workflow, authority and accountability redesign will produce diminishing returns.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2106914993526747224

## Custom GPT migration exposes platform risk
Mollick said a widely used GPT can move to a private plugin, but not retain its public purpose.
Companies and educators invested years in the format.
The lesson is to own instructions, knowledge and tests rather than a temporary interface.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2106958444196413670

## One-shot software gets more capable
Mollick shared a city-builder generated from one prompt with Fable 5.1.
The example suggests polished creation is becoming easier and faster.
The differentiator moves from making a demo to maintaining a reliable business system.
