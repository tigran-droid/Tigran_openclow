# Daily news — 2026-09-24

## Real tasks expose the wrong model metrics
A 12-task comparison tested Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra on websites, video, spreadsheets, presentations and browser work. Actual cost and speed varied sharply by task despite Astra's higher listed rate. Benchmark the work, not the price per token.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GmLcJVzkxPA

## AI finds a biological lead humans missed
Claude agents reportedly searched 1.9 billion protein clusters, narrowed 200,000 candidates to 20 and identified an unusual enzyme system supported by lab evidence. Ten repeat searches missed it, making reproducibility central.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2102872138596163979

## Capability costs keep collapsing
The cost of reaching strong scores on difficult math and science benchmarks is falling while ability increases. Long procurement cycles risk optimizing for a cost curve that has moved.
