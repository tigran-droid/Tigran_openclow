# Daily news — 2026-09-16
## The trillion-dollar productivity test
Hyperscalers could spend nearly $1.1 trillion through 2027.
One estimate says they need 2.7 times their own productivity by 2030 to justify it.
The business case now depends on customers producing measurable value, not more pilots.
Source: https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/09/15/1144028/ai-infrastructure-boom-investment-bubble-risk/

## Seven hours saved, more checking
A Google study highlighted by Ethan Mollick reports scientists saving about seven hours per week with AI.
The work also shifts toward verification and may steer researchers toward safer topics.
Time saved is only useful if organisations redesign review and decision-making around it.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2100102316447670677

## Claude moves into Salesforce
Salesforce in Claude entered beta with 37 pre-built sales skills.
The system can work with accounts, opportunities and forecasts while routing actions through existing permissions.
Conversational access is becoming a new front end for core business systems.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2099884035119960217

## Voice agents get cheaper
Google introduced two Gemini Live voice models, including a lower-cost version reported at about $0.84 per hour of input audio.
The models reportedly support 97 languages, tool calls and visual grounding.
If independent testing confirms the claims, longer customer-service and onboarding workflows become economically practical.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2099971300953333912

## Pacing becomes political
AI labs, policymakers and independent evaluators are debating temporary limits on frontier development.
The hard question is who gets access to evidence, who can enforce a boundary and what happens when a lab refuses.
