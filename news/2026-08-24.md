# Daily news — 2026-08-24
## Open harness challenges the defaults
Nate Herk tested the open-source DeepSeek Harness for roughly 100 hours.
Using the same model, it beat Claude Code on speed in two tasks but produced less trustworthy client-ready research.
The useful comparison is cost and quality at completion, not model name or token price.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UsfCe5fJK6A

## AI should invent workflows, not copy them
The AI Daily Brief examined arguments about work after automation.
The strongest business claim was that companies will lose if they merely add AI to existing workflows.
Efficiency is a start; opportunity comes from work that was not previously possible.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m8x-gYMYFCg

## The organisation needs a faster clock
Agents act in seconds while teams, finance and leadership work on weekly, quarterly and annual rhythms.
A continuously updated record of goals, decisions, permissions and constraints could synchronize work.
Shared status still cannot replace human alignment and judgment.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m8x-gYMYFCg

## Research claims age differently
Ethan Mollick argued that positive capability findings from older models often remain useful.
Negative findings age faster because newer models may remove the limitation.
Leaders should inspect the model, date and durability of the claimed mechanism.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2091562657623265727

## Compute is becoming a traded input
A compute marketplace is being built around unused capacity and daily GPU-hour pricing.
Its ambition is to create indices and futures markets for enterprise compute.
Volatile unit prices make workload economics and portability more important.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aGdAgUo_BgY
