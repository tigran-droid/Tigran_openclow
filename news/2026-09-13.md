# Daily news — 2026-09-13

## Long-running agents cross a new threshold
OpenAI reported 3.1 agent workdays for every human research workday.
Its own figures say Astra completed 40% of one-to-two-day tasks without intervention and nearly 90% with help.
These are company-reported measures, but they move the management question from prompting to supervision.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqQ5kIwe1nQ

## Experts can see what demos conceal
Ethan Mollick says Astra and Fable can research and write credible academic papers.
The weakness is now difficult for a non-expert to detect.
Businesses need domain specialists in evaluation, not only enthusiastic users scoring fluent output.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2099011701949583614

## Independent evaluation gains momentum
Sam Altman backed independent evaluators with employee-like access after Dario Amodei called for pacing the frontier.
Apollo Research reportedly had only three days with Astra before release.
Early, embedded scrutiny matters more than a last-minute external review.
Source: https://x.com/sama/status/2098811563415150910

## Capacity becomes a product constraint
OpenAI paused new subscriptions to its $200 Pro plan after unprecedented Astra demand.
Microsoft reportedly plans to grow global data-centre capacity from 12 to 38 gigawatts by 2032.
AI budgets now need usage scenarios and capacity risk, not just seat prices.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y2hE6kYZPg0

## Agents enter editable production tools
New creative systems claim to work directly inside After Effects, Blender and Photoshop rather than returning flat outputs.
The useful shift is editability: teams can inspect, change and continue the work.
That is a better procurement test than the quality of a one-shot demo.
