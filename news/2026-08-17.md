# Daily news — 2026-08-17

## AI learns by watching the work
ChatGPT computer history observes interaction events across work, while Grok Bot lets a user deliberately record a task.
Both approaches try to solve the context gap without requiring a perfect written prompt.
For businesses, process demonstration is becoming a practical route into automation.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtnZzy6tERA

## A simple test for AI delegation
A deputization audit scores recurring work across frequency, teachability, checkability, stakes and dependence on the individual.
Tasks scoring eight to ten are candidates for delegation; zero to three should stay with the person; the middle becomes a human-AI duet.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtnZzy6tERA

## Token price fails the real cost test
An AlphaSense study tested models on several hundred financial-analysis questions using filings, earnings calls and news.
It reported GPT-5.6 Soul completing the work around 13% cheaper than Kimi K3 while scoring around 20% higher on quality.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtnZzy6tERA

## Qualitative work still needs evaluation
Ethan Mollick argued that writing, ideas and pitches are already judged through structured human opinion in the real world.
Established qualitative research methods can evaluate these outputs without pretending they have automatic answer keys.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2089042815405686919

## Agents now get different kinds of computers
Local coding agents work on a user's machine, some web agents receive a temporary machine that resets, and Grok Bot gives agents a persistent web machine.
These architectures create different privacy, continuity and recovery trade-offs.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2089233231853785118
