# Daily news — 2026-08-11

## Finance moves beyond the spreadsheet
OpenAI’s finance team is working towards a zero-day close and continuously updated forecasting.
Its practical model starts with a consequential decision, then maps the data, approvals and handoffs behind it.
The team says investor diligence drafts that took hours can now be produced in seconds, with finance still owning review and sign-off.
Source: https://openai.com/index/building-an-ai-native-finance-function

## AI consulting gets a service ladder
Nate Herk framed AI consulting as education, audit, project, then retainer.
The useful part is not the sales formula. It is the insistence that consultants first build a workflow they use themselves and prove one measurable result before expanding.
That replaces abstract AI advice with evidence a client can inspect.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LVAHYV4Xrto

## Human approval is not automatically safer
The AI Daily Brief reported Anthropic’s findings from more than 1,000 testers of Claude Code auto mode.
The automated control caught 89% of harmful actions, while human reviewers caught 13.6%.
Users approved 97% of proposed changes, exposing how quickly frequent approval prompts become theatre.
Anthropic says auto-mode users ship 25% more pull requests, though the figures remain company-reported.
