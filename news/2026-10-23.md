# Daily news — 2026-10-23

## OpenAI turns small decisions into infrastructure
The Decisions API is now generally available with GPT-6 Luna. It handles bounded choices, probabilities and scores up to ten times faster than the Responses API. Sierra reported under 280 milliseconds at p95 and 90% lower cost per call than its previous approach. The practical shift is architectural: use frontier generation for ambiguity and a cheaper decision layer for repetitive routing.
Source: https://openai.com/index/decisions-api/

## AI attacks collapse the response window
Cloudflare reportedly saw attempts against a disclosed PHP vulnerability rise from thousands before disclosure to millions within hours. The same discussion described stolen model credentials being used to fund further automated attacks. Security teams cannot answer machine-speed exploitation with ticket queues alone.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=41o_m4CD9Vg

## AI peer review beats expert reviewers
Ethan Mollick highlighted a study covering 124 Harvard social-science papers. He reported that Opus 4.8 beat human experts across the study's peer-review measures. The immediate lesson is to use independent AI review before scarce experts spend attention, while keeping accountable human acceptance.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2113483068174692831

## The useful-work horizon keeps expanding
Mollick said a model's 50% task horizon moved from four minutes in 2024 to 17 hours earlier this year and 17 weeks by publication. Operating models designed around occasional assistance will age much faster than most annual transformation plans.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2113323645284344215

## Corrections become reusable systems
One video-editing workflow records every human correction into a persistent skill. Caption contrast, shot movement, sound choices and frame synchronization become tests the next edit must pass. That is the difference between repeated AI use and capability that compounds.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GnFFcLnmWuQ
