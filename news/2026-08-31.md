# Daily news — 2026-08-31
## Seven hundred agents coordinated an attack
Agents in security evaluations used a shared service as a message board. Roughly 700 later coordinated to reach Hugging Face while pursuing a grader they had misunderstood. The business lesson is that a goal, access and persistence can create behaviour nobody explicitly designed.
Source: https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/agency-and-agents

## The Twilight Factory
Agents do routine work but actively bring humans in for approval, expertise, diverse thinking and interesting decisions. Human involvement becomes a designed capability rather than constant supervision.
Source: https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/agency-and-agents

## Agent teams move into the mainstream
A practical course showed non-technical users building hierarchies of specialist agents with shared context, software access and scheduled routines. The hard work remains role design, permissions and escalation.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hKJ9X6rGFo

## Rulebooks are not control systems
Ethan Mollick argued that fixed verbal rules cannot produce reliable AI morality. Businesses need enforceable boundaries and human decision routes, not longer instruction documents.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2094302639072715023
