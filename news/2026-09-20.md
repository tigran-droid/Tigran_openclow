# Daily news — 2026-09-20

## AI takes on a century-old mathematics problem
OpenAI says an internal system produced a proposed solution to the Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize problem.
Roughly 10,000 agents worked for 88 hours, followed by 17 hours of formalisation in Lean.
The claim still needs independent scrutiny, while a dispute over parallel human work raises provenance questions.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pBRekQT8JUQ

## A model built only to decide
Jev returns yes-or-no judgments, categories and scores with confidence levels rather than prose.
Its makers claim it is 20 to 200 times faster and 40 to 400 times cheaper than general models.
Early testing points to cheap routing before expensive reasoning, though results were uneven.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ymgH8jS6Wb8

## Skills become operating infrastructure
A practical Codex demonstration showed how repeatable work can be packaged as reusable skills.
The useful method begins with a definition of done, reverse-engineers the process, then tests the result.
