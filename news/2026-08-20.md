# Daily news — 2026-08-20

## Five skills for AI-managed work
The AI Daily Brief proposed a practical map for knowledge workers.
It starts with domain judgment, then adds capability mapping, context and harness management, prototyping, opportunity identification and rapid learning.
The implication is that AI training should be built around real work, not feature tours.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f40AKMHebrY

## Product managers build before the meeting
A mid-sized ecommerce CTO reportedly now sees product managers bring working Replit demos directly into feature discussions.
Work that once waited roughly three months for developer attention can be demonstrated immediately.
The business shift is faster validation, not the fantasy that every prototype is production-ready.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iDQ-vSamQeU

## Model switching exposes weak evaluations
Lindy moved its virtual teammate towards DeepSeek after frontier-model costs became difficult to sustain.
An earlier model passed the company’s tests, yet users said the product had become stupid.
That gap is the warning: an evaluation suite is only useful if it captures what customers actually notice.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iDQ-vSamQeU

## Agents get eyes inside VR
Meta’s experimental XR Operator exposes screenshots, spatial state, controller input and scene information to compatible agents.
The stated goal is a build-test-verify loop inside a running VR application.
The useful advance is not more generated code. It is evidence that the code works in the environment a person will experience.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2090080648383934553

## Privacy becomes a product capability
OpenAI reaffirmed Zero Data Retention for eligible API customers and previewed Private Safety Processing.
The offer is aimed at applying advanced safety checks without retaining sensitive customer content.
For enterprise buyers, privacy architecture is becoming part of model capability, not a legal footnote.
Source: https://openai.com/index/offering-zero-data-retention-for-frontier-models
