# Daily news — 2026-09-02
## OpenAI classifies Astra as critical cyber capability
OpenAI says Astra can find previously unknown vulnerabilities and develop working exploits across hardened systems.
It scored 100% on ExploitBench and found two previously unknown V8 vulnerabilities during an internal evaluation.
Advanced cyber access will be limited, with monitoring able to pause or stop work.
Source: https://openai.com/index/path-to-astra/

## Anthropic makes long-running work cheaper
Anthropic says Fable 5.1 will cost about 25% less than Fable 5 for typical workloads.
For highly agentic work, it estimates savings of up to 45% through lower cache-read pricing.
The business case for persistent agents is moving from capability alone to cost per completed task.
Source: https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1

## Fable 5.1 improves on business workflows
Anthropic reports an AutomationBench score of 31.4%, up from 17.1% for Fable 5.
Early customers describe unattended runs lasting hours and, in one case, 38 hours.
These are vendor and customer claims, but they point to a change in the unit of work leaders should evaluate.
Source: https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1

## Enterprise safeguards move into customer infrastructure
Anthropic says its new enterprise safeguards keep customer data in cloud infrastructure controlled by the customer.
The system was developed with more than 100 organisations across regulated industries.
This could reduce the old trade-off between strong monitoring and zero data retention.
Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/enterprise-frontier-safeguards

## Video analysis learns to skip
Google says Gemini can choose which parts of a video to inspect instead of processing every frame.
The company claims token use can fall by as much as 88% on long-form video tasks.
Selective attention makes recorded meetings, training and research libraries more realistic operational inputs.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2094856920725131355
