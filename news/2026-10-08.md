# Daily news — 2026-10-08

## Chat answers become interfaces
OpenAI says Intelligent UI can generate charts, maps, forms and small interactive tools inside a response.
GPT-6 is also being extended across paid and free ChatGPT tiers.
The important shift is from producing text to assembling the right interface for the task.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2108094193067307209

## Small models get materially cheaper
Anthropic says Claude Haiku 5.5 costs about 75% less on average than Haiku 4.5.
For prompts up to 100,000 tokens, it lists $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens.
That changes the economics of high-volume background work.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2108096885898490141

## Consumer AI spending stays concentrated
Only 2.2% of US households paid for an AI subscription as of April, according to research discussed by The AI Daily Brief.
Among paying users, the top 1% spend $903 a month on average while the median buyer spends $25.
The next problem is creating value ordinary customers will repeatedly pay for.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J3n4IUwmuOM

## Enterprise software velocity jumps
OutSystems says it moved from four major feature releases in one quarter to 19 and then 26.
Customers are reconsidering some six-year legacy projects as possible six-month programmes.
The constraint shifts from code production to architecture, permissions, compliance and acceptance.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y2qs-BKoMb4

## Software creation becomes conversational
Google Labs is testing Playground, where users describe a game and create a browser-based prototype without coding.
The experiment lowers the first barrier to interactive software creation from syntax to clear intent.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2107893954540663015
