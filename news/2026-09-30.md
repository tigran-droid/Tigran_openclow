# Daily news — 2026-09-30

## OpenAI pushes the price floor down
OpenAI says GPT-6.1 Sol offers near-Astra capability at one-fifth of Astra's standard input and output prices.
Sam Altman also highlighted a 95% cache-read discount.
The procurement question is shifting from which model is best to which model is sufficient for each task.
Source: https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-1-sol

## Persistent assistants multiply
OpenAI introduced Dots as assistants that work continuously across projects.
Microsoft described Autopilot as a persistent agent that monitors channels, follows up and runs recurring work.
The category is moving from answering requests to maintaining responsibility over time.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vJl6AaQ6B0A

## Custom GPT workflows face migration
OpenAI plans to retire affected custom GPTs and move users toward plugins.
Instructions and knowledge can migrate, but selected models, existing conversations and custom actions do not transfer automatically.
Businesses now need migration tests for AI workflows, not just software backups.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vJl6AaQ6B0A

## Team agents need shared operations
The AI Daily Brief examined the shift from personal agents to agents used by teams.
Shared context, ownership, permissions and maintenance become harder once an agent serves several people.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gzt74VA8J5Q

## Efficiency is not customer value
A Havas study covering more than 21,000 evaluations found only 9% of branded AI experiences reached its highest value score.
Cost reduction without utility, credibility and appeal can weaken the experience it was meant to improve.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2104898952344543585

## Optimism still exceeds trust
A Gallup study with Microsoft covered 37 countries. Median AI awareness was 81%, but only 43% had used it and 36% expressed high trust in its accuracy.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2104860756818760051
