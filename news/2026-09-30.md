# Daily news — 2026-09-30

## OpenAI gives agents their own computers
OpenAI introduced Dots, always-on agents with a cloud computer and browser.
They can be reached through ChatGPT, Slack, Teams or voice and connect to thousands of apps.
This moves AI from answering requests to carrying work between requests.
For businesses, permissions and stop conditions now matter as much as prompt quality.
Source: https://x.com/sama/status/2104995014208258235

## Shared AI gets a workplace
ChatGPT Space brings people, pages, files and agents into one shared working area.
Pages can be updated by ChatGPT, Codex or a Dot instead of becoming static documents.
The opportunity is living operational context rather than another document repository.
The risk is a new layer of overlapping products that employees must somehow understand.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2105177363231220100

## Frontier capability gets cheaper
OpenAI says GPT-6.1 Sol costs one fifth of Astra, with a 95 percent cache-read discount.
Anthropic says Sonnet 5.5 completes typical work with up to 30 percent lower task cost.
The buying question is shifting from token price to the cost of an accepted business outcome.
Cheaper intelligence makes workflow design, evaluation and routing more valuable.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vJl6AaQ6B0A

## Team agents replace private experiments
AI-forward companies are moving from one personal agent per employee to fewer shared agents.
Every reportedly made that shift within months, while Sierra combined specialist agents.
Shared agents need a named owner, maintained knowledge and clear permission boundaries.
Without those, a team agent becomes shared confusion at machine speed.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gzt74VA8J5Q

## AI sentiment splits down the middle
An informal survey of nearly 300 AI-focused respondents found 25 percent more excited.
At the same time, 27 percent were more cautious and 25 percent more worried.
Adoption plans that assume enthusiasm will fail with half the workforce.
Transformation needs room for concern, evidence and visible human control.
