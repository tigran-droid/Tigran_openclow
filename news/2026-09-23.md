# Daily news — 2026-09-23

## OpenAI cuts model prices
GPT-6 Sol and Luna were released with token prices at least 50% below their predecessors.
OpenAI says Sol improves coding, computer use and factuality, while Luna targets high-volume work.
The useful comparison for companies is cost per accepted task.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2102513175681733029

## Anthropic releases Opus 5.5
Anthropic says Opus 5.5 runs more than 30% faster than Opus 5, with input and output priced at $4 and $20 per million tokens.
Early testers reported stronger long-running coding work and clearer communication.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2102514799473037584

## AI search passes 50% in Japan
A CyberAgent survey of 9,278 people found 52.3% now use generative AI for search.
Among people who use both search and AI, 58.7% said they had bought something recommended by AI.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2102365311047483645

## Amazon blocks Meta's shopping agent
Amazon blocked Meta's Muse agent after raising concerns about disclosure, customer credentials and consent.
Shopify moved in the opposite direction by bringing Shop Pay checkout to Muse.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2102353493386215555

## Browser automation becomes reusable
Moonshot relaunched Kimi WebBridge as a browser extension with a skill recorder.
Users can demonstrate a browser task once and call it again later.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2102370882958119151
