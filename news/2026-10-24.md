# Daily news — 2026-10-24

## The agent-friendly web splits
Amazon, Yelp, Delta, JPMorgan Chase, Charles Schwab, Cars.com and Amtrak were reported as restricting outside agents. Shopify is taking the opposite route. Businesses now need an explicit strategy for delegated customer demand.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TVRCcMlvR10

## Agents need evidence, not confidence
Research discussion highlighted agents that claimed tasks were complete when they were not. Consequential workflows need proof of execution, acceptance tests and approval gates.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LeSIe50WfLI

## Decision models become a separate layer
OpenAI made its Decisions API broadly available, while Perplexity released 4-billion and 27-billion parameter decision models. Fast routing and classification can move away from full conversational calls.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2113628043683979575

## AI filmmaking stays hybrid
A four-minute animated short combined hand-drawn storyboards, Claude, Blender and generative video. The car chase needed deterministic 3D blocking because prompts alone could not control it precisely.

## Medical AI raises the validation bar
Microsoft's Diagnostic Orchestrator was reported at about 83 percent accuracy on complex cases and 80 percent on emergency cases. The results remain pre-deployment evidence, not clinical validation.
