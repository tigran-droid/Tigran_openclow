# Daily news — 2026-10-16

## The agent adoption gap
McKinsey says 40% of large companies now have AI agents running.
Only 6% report that AI contributes even 5% to earnings.
The proposed answer is an AI operating system that owns company context while renting changing technology.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K70Qf06XBLE

## AI champions become an operating role
One enterprise practitioner called a structured champion network the highest-ROI AI transformation activity she has seen.
The role spans use-case selection, evaluation, deployment and value tracking.
Without manager support, protected time and ongoing ownership, trained champions return to their old jobs.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wdXF-5nn-T4

## Frontier quality compounds
Ethan Mollick highlighted research showing that small gains in model accuracy and judgment can create large differences across long tasks.
His implication is to keep stronger models in orchestration roles rather than replacing them everywhere with cheaper ones.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2110918113550307778

## Old benchmarks are breaking
Mollick argued that model harnesses and stronger models are exhausting several famous tests.
Long-duration tasks, professional document production and novel puzzle solving are moving beyond yesterday’s evaluation ceiling.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2110875851600633956

## Open image models move onto local hardware
Black Forest Labs released FLUX 3 Image Klein with open weights and versions designed for machines with 8 GB of memory.
The company claims native 4K output, editing, up to 10 reference images and API pricing from $0.009 per 1K image.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2110821477788324062
