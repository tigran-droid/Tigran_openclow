# Daily news — 2026-10-04

## Enterprise AI gets a management layer
A KPMG survey covered more than 2,100 senior leaders across 20 countries.
Among organisations with established AI returns, 86% have a formal AI harness layer and 53% have an enterprise-wide sovereignty strategy.
The lesson is not to buy more models. It is to build the operating system around them.

## Agent adoption splits the field
Significant employee adoption of AI agents reached 48% among organisations with established returns.
It was only 15% among firms still experimenting.
The divide is moving from access to operational capability.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rx7G-8myrd8

## AI economics become measurable
Seventy-seven per cent of established-return organisations have AI cost-monitoring dashboards, and 48% consistently compare value with cost.
Average planned investment rose from $186 million in the first quarter to $210 million in the third.
Serious adopters are connecting spend to outcomes.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rx7G-8myrd8

## Robotics remains in its GPT-2 era
Google DeepMind's robotics lead described the field as early despite striking humanoid demonstrations.
Running fast on a flat track is easier than handling varied objects reliably in the real world.
Businesses should test task reliability and recovery, not buy the theatre of a humanoid form.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CVcyli4i5g0

## Cheap video becomes workflow infrastructure
HeyGen says its new video model costs $0.02 per second, temporarily reduced to $0.01, with generated visuals and sound in one call.
The figures are vendor claims and still need independent testing.
If the economics hold, video becomes a repeatable function inside a larger workflow.
