# Daily news — 2026-10-07

## Contract agents get a real test
OpenAI and Ironclad built 11 legal, commercial and procurement tasks.
GPT-6 Astra averaged 55.0% against detailed rubrics, up from 41.6% for GPT-5.6 Sol.
Estimated attempt time fell from 37.0 to 19.2 minutes.
The result is progress, not production readiness: nearly half the criteria were still missed.
Source: https://openai.com/index/advancing-computer-use-with-ironclad

## AI produces new mathematics
OpenAI published a set of mathematical results from an internal frontier model.
Many proofs were formalised in Lean so a computer can check them.
The average accepted result used roughly three hours of ChatGPT Pro-equivalent thinking.
Scientific output is getting cheaper; verification and attribution now become the scarce layer.
Source: https://openai.com/index/sharing-ai-progress-in-mathematics

## Quant research moves beyond code snippets
Jump Trading says GPT-6 Astra can run multi-day research workflows across several data sources.
Humans still define the environment, goals, evaluation criteria and final acceptance.
The practical lesson is not “remove the researcher.”
It is “give the agent a measurable problem and a controlled place to work.”
Source: https://openai.com/index/jump-trading

## Personal agents hit the trust wall
OpenAI’s Dots are designed to work continuously across connected applications.
That usefulness depends on access to calendars, files, messages and other sensitive context.
The Artificial Intelligence Show argued that most organisations still struggle with basic assistant adoption.
The adoption problem is moving from prompting to permission, trust and sustained use.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X-JA9VVnUBs

## AI begins to audit science
Ethan Mollick expects published research to be re-read and re-judged at machine scale.
The Cognitive Revolution discussed a related prospect: faster discovery paired with large-scale challenges to existing work.
The risk is treating model confidence as scientific verdict.
Institutions will need evidence standards before automated review becomes automated accusation.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBGz-1Mqorw
