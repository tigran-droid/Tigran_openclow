# Daily news — 2026-09-25

## Model competition shifts to efficiency
Anthropic says Opus 5.5 costs $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens, with lower operating cost and faster generation than Opus 5. OpenAI says Sol and Luna are priced 50% below GPT-5.6 promotional pricing. The business question is which model and setting completes a real task at the lowest accepted cost.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KYGscdtRS1Y

## More effort did not win
A six-run test used the same 3D conference task at every Opus 5.5 effort level. Max ran for 2 hours 28 minutes and would have cost $50.38, but the tester preferred Extra at roughly half the time and cost.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCkHIyEPIYo

## A 141-hour agent forgot how to recover
GPT-6 Astra reportedly made record progress in Minecraft through ordinary computer controls. After a Creeper destroyed its stored items and reset point, the agent spent hours farming potatoes rather than rebuilding the plan.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2103121873621938370

## Smart-glasses privacy gets an opt-out
Meta says users can stop visual data from its smart glasses being used for training, but the control does not cover audio interactions. Enterprise policy must distinguish every data type, retention path and review route.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2103161493105655873

## Software moves behind the conversation
Adobe says Acrobat editing is joining Claude while Photoshop, Lightroom, Express and Firefly tools are coming into Gemini. The chat becomes the front door, increasing the importance of permissions, evidence and ownership.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2103164630101008463

## Multi-agent testing gets a shared world
Odyssey says Agora-2 supports four human controllers and sixteen autonomous agents in a shared generated environment. The practical promise is controlled observation of emergent behaviour before multi-agent systems reach production.
