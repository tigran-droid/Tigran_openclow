# Daily news — 2026-08-12
## AI use is mainstream. Measured return is not.
Gallup puts workplace AI use at 52% of US workers.
Yet one enterprise study found 57% saying return still failed to outpace spend.
The consulting problem has moved from adoption to measurable operating change.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Laws8go7v98

## The hidden AI workforce
PagerDuty found 66% of office professionals had used AI they believed violated company policy.
An Atlassian experiment found disclosed AI users were rated ten times lazier than identical peers who stayed quiet.
Bad policy and bad culture are driving useful practice underground.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Laws8go7v98

## Managing agents becomes the job
BCG found 47% of workers spending more time managing and supervising AI than doing what they called actual work.
OpenAI reported that more than 25% of Codex users had delegated a task estimated at over eight hours of human work.
Companies need agent-management skills, not another generic prompt workshop.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Laws8go7v98

## Cyber models enter the existing control plane
OpenAI made Daybreak Blue and Daybreak Red available to eligible customers through Amazon Bedrock.
The models support defensive work, vulnerability research, exploit validation and mitigation development.
The important change is deployment inside familiar procurement, access-control and governance workflows.
Source: https://openai.com/index/daybreak-models-are-now-available-on-aws

## ChatGPT ads expand internationally
OpenAI launched ChatGPT Ads in the United Kingdom, Mexico, Brazil, Japan and South Korea.
Ads remain outside Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education tiers.
Conversational discovery is becoming a commercial channel, with trust and answer independence as the central risk.
Source: https://openai.com/index/testing-ads-in-chatgpt
