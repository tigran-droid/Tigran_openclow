## Agentic loops move into knowledge work
A new workshop showed knowledge workers how to define verifiable finish lines, hard turn caps and safe working boundaries for long-running agents.
It also warned that loops can burn budget, stall without progress or satisfy a weak specification with mediocre work.
The business skill is defining completion, not writing a longer prompt.
