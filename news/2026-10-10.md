# Daily news — 2026-10-10

## Microsoft builds a model only to decide
Microsoft says Decision-1 handles fixed choices, scores and yes-or-no judgments rather than prose.
It claims 4.5x lower latency than the next-fastest decision model and 35x lower latency than GPT-6 Sol.
Pricing is $0.042 per million input tokens, with output tokens free.
Routine agent routing and checking may no longer need a general-purpose model.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2108681185543032851

## Cheap models move into the agent stack
Anthropic positions Haiku 5.5 for high-volume tasks and sub-agents.
Artificial Analysis put its cost at about $0.21 per task at maximum inference.
The architectural lesson is to reserve expensive reasoning for the few steps that need it.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=thO4Y5RYfHo

## Multi-model products become normal
Grokbot says it will choose the best back-end model for each task, including third-party models.
Ramp data also showed model routers gaining ground among early-adopter businesses.
The durable asset shifts toward routing logic, context and evaluations.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=thO4Y5RYfHo

## Refusal is not a safety boundary
Model refusals remain probabilistic, vulnerable to jailbreaks and prone to blocking legitimate work.
Anthropic has said one classifier layer added 24% to chatbot compute costs.
Consequential workflows need permissions and external controls outside the model.
Source: https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/10/09/1145728/we-are-putting-too-much-faith-in-ai-to-say-no/

## Automation does not guarantee learning
Randomised trials found that AI raised test scores.
Some gains persisted when students used AI as a tutor, but faded when AI wrote for them.
The distinction between assistance and substitution matters at work too.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2108594749305135613
