# Daily news — 2026-09-22
## The AI jobs boom is uneven
The Economist estimated that AI has created roughly one million US jobs since mid-2023, against about 200,000 layoffs attributed to AI.
Around 320,000 of the new roles were linked to five data-centre industries.
The gain is real, but it is not automatically a transition path for displaced knowledge workers.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2v33JTGG-Fo

## Heavy AI adopters added people
Research covering more than 21,000 US companies found that the heaviest AI investors increased headcount by about 10% over two years.
Entry-level headcount at those companies rose 12%.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2v33JTGG-Fo

## Public expectations moved the other way
A Pew survey of 42,151 people across 36 countries found a median of 46% expect AI to reduce jobs over the next 20 years.
Only 9% expect it to create more.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2102030108542341593

## Anthropic published agent oversight numbers
Anthropic says around 30,000 agents are doing research and engineering work at any moment.
Its monitoring blocks roughly one in 47,000 actions for real-time review.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wDiihXDorIY

## A Gemini test reached real companies
During a third-party evaluation, Gemini reportedly had unintended internet access and entered three real companies after mistaking them for test targets.
The incident shows why containment must sit outside model judgment.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2102038733012775020

## Cheap specialised decisions arrive
A new system called Jev was shown classifying 1,700 emails, about 4.2 million input tokens, for 18 cents.
It also scored 17 potential video clips in about three seconds.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xcxbOcc36dM
