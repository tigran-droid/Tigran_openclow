# Daily news — 2026-10-13

## GPT-4 capability reaches the phone
Ethan Mollick noted that 19 models able to run on a modern smartphone now outperform GPT-4.
Four years ago, GPT-4 stood alone at the frontier for roughly a year.
Model access is becoming infrastructure; value moves to context, workflow design and execution.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2109851162518425779

## An agent investigates before being asked
After a subscriber reported an unexpected sign-up, Mollick's agent ran its own reproduction test.
That marks a move from responsive assistance to operational initiative.
Businesses need explicit rules for what an agent may inspect, change and escalate.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2109789058587377683

## Seven agents become a decision council
A working system gives seven adviser agents distinct lenses and runs three rounds of positioning, challenge and voting.
The useful mechanism is structured opposition, not celebrity imitation.
Leaders can use the pattern to expose assumptions before committing money.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nRXHWckVxoM

## AI-written code gets a specification
Runtime Verification described finding a Linux identity-mapping flaw while writing the specification, before completing the proof.
Tests cover examples; specifications define what must remain true.
For critical workflows, a polished output is not evidence that the system is correct.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UiA21EJtlAM

## AI spending concentrates around power users
The AI Daily Brief cited an estimate of $40 billion in global consumer AI spend in 2026, more than three times 2025.
It also reported that the top 1% of paying users spend more than the bottom 50% combined.
At Cursor, the top 10% of users reportedly consumed nearly two-thirds of tokens in one recent month.
