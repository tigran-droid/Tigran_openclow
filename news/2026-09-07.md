## Real work exposes model trade-offs
Astra beat Fable 5.1 on 10 of 15 practical tasks in one hands-on comparison.
Total reported cost was $326.98 against $513.36, but Astra took one hour and 43 minutes longer overall.
Model choice should be made per workflow using accepted output, cost and elapsed time.

## Expertise becomes more valuable
Ethan Mollick argued that experts find the jagged frontier faster and judge output quality better.
Non-experts are more likely to accept defaults because they have fewer ways to diagnose weak work.
Domain judgment must be connected to building and evaluation.
