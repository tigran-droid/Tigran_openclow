## Five days of autonomous knowledge work
Ethan Mollick says Astra built a multi-gigabyte personal wiki from his emails, writing and calendar over five uninterrupted days.
It now reviews new email against that context twice daily.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2095606622055760159
