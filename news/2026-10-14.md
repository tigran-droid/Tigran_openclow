## Google returns to the frontier
Gemini 4 Argon is available across Google's paid products and API.
It offers up to one million output tokens with introductory API pricing of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens.
Hands-on testing found strong coding and creative work, but task mode stalled on basic research and slide-generation jobs.

## AI code breaks human-speed infrastructure
Anthropic reportedly grew from roughly 2,500 code changes a day in May to more than 30,000 by September.
It built Scion with a seven-person team and migrated its monorepo in five months.
Reported results included infrastructure about 200 times lighter and a 25% CPU capacity gain for continuous integration.

## Failed startups become training data
Companies winding down are selling code, email, chat and workspace archives to AI labs and data vendors.
SimpleClosure says 47% of its deals now include code or data sales and expects to return more than $10 million by the end of 2026.
The unresolved issues are employee consent, private information and who receives the proceeds.
Source: https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/10/13/1145755/ai-labs-buying-data-from-dead-startups-to-train-models/

## Open models force a business trade-off
Open models can be cheaper, run locally and keep sensitive work in a company's own environment.
They also make powerful capabilities easier to modify and misuse.
Businesses need workload-based policies rather than an ideological answer to open versus closed.
Source: https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/10/13/1146366/open-models-have-real-benefits-do-they-outweigh-the-risks/
