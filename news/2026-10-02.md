# Daily news — 2026-10-02

## Alibaba turns B2B into agent-to-agent commerce
Alibaba.com says more than 10 million small businesses use Accio Work and consumed one trillion AI tokens over a recent three-month period. The platform is attacking a sourcing process that can involve around 60 steps and orders averaging about $3,000.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTLdkUZVpEk

## Agents organise themselves
Ethan Mollick argued that better models are reducing the need to hand-design agent org charts. He cited a research swarm that exchanged about 2.7 million messages and reached a result after 88 hours.
Source: https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/the-dot-and-the-swarm

## Model benchmarks meet workflow economics
Anthropic says Sonnet 5.5 is 30% faster and 30% cheaper than Sonnet 5, while independent maximum-setting testing cited by The AI Daily Brief found a $7.60 cost per task.

## Accounting crosses a capability threshold
