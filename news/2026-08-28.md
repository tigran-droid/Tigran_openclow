# Daily news — 2026-08-28

## HubSpot rebuilds the customer journey
HubSpot says more than 80% of its website chats are handled by its sales bot.
Its answer-engine optimisation conversions reportedly rose almost 2,000% in recent months.
The method is to map the customer journey, identify constraints and redesign one stage at a time.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCBKRp1jEMA

## Automation is not augmentation
Research highlighted by Ethan Mollick suggests models that complete tasks well do not always help humans perform them better.
Businesses must decide whether a workflow removes work, improves judgment or does both.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2093045682785378666

## AI buyers remain unpredictable
Agentic-shopping research found that page order and agent memory changed purchasing preferences.
Companies preparing for machine customers need controlled experiments rather than assumptions about rational buyers.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2092994716954394999

## Bad ideas spread across agent swarms
A demonstrated agent mind virus can persist in memory and pass from one agent to another.
Independent checks, diversity and reversible execution are becoming operating requirements.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVqM3f9Izro

## AI cyber defence enters an urgent phase
Sam Altman called for an urgent collective response to AI-enabled cyber risk.
Companies should treat AI security as an active operating capability, not an annual policy exercise.
Source: https://x.com/sama/status/2093060670472241368
