# Daily news — 2026-09-11

## A bank builds its own AI workforce
A 700-person private bank described 29 AI champions and roughly 100 completed projects. An internal application cut account-review time by 50% and replaced a product priced at $375,000 a year. Its IT agent is already closing work at roughly half the rate of a full-time employee.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IIHsCFPo2zY
