# Daily news — 2026-10-09

## Claude Code cuts instruction baggage
Anthropic engineers reportedly removed more than 80% of the system prompt for newer Claude models and reported no measurable evaluation loss. The practical lesson is to delete old rules that no longer prove their value.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oz2CwrPV2Rg

## Agent testing moves inside real companies
Mercor is building environments from acquired companies’ real Salesforce, Slack and Figma data. Agents receive roles, relationships and messy organisational demands rather than one neat task.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FOwFk7LWCsA

## OpenAI sells latency as a premium
OpenAI says GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast can run up to eight times faster than Sol Standard, at roughly six times the reported API price. Speed now needs its own business case.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2108292616760479923

## Organisations remain the missing layer
Ethan Mollick argued that organisations are themselves a form of narrow superintelligence and that weak integration may explain limited productivity impact.

## AI advice reaches a clinical threshold
