# Daily news — 2026-10-03

## Proactive AI reaches the inbox
OpenAI’s Dots can monitor connected email, Slack and calendars, then suggest or take action. Early access testing showed it drafting replies, archiving messages and assembling a daily agenda without a fresh prompt. Access starts on a $100 monthly plan, making this a test of whether businesses will pay for initiative, not merely intelligence.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dDgncbBAA0c

## Cheap models, expensive settings
GPT-6.1 Sol is priced at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, one fifth of Astra’s listed rates. Claude Sonnet 5.5 is cheaper than Opus at standard settings, but one test found its maximum-effort configuration cost more per task. Model procurement now needs workflow tests at the exact effort level teams will actually use.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dDgncbBAA0c

## OpenAI publishes a production guide
OpenAI’s new GPT-6 guide puts caching, compaction, steering, asynchronous tools and delegation at the centre of production deployment. It also says prompts, skills and repository instructions must agree on scope and what counts as done.
Source: https://openai.com/index/practical-guide-building-gpt-6

## Fluent is not the same as reasoning
MIT Technology Review argues that longer chains of text are still produced by next-token prediction, unlike AlphaGo’s separate search mechanism. Businesses need external checks whenever an answer must be correct and defensible rather than merely plausible.
Source: https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/10/02/1145639/dont-be-fooled-llms-dont-reason/

## Junior hiring signal appears, but remains contested
Ethan Mollick highlighted evidence that AI may already be affecting hiring at the margin in exposed junior white-collar roles. The same analysis says aggregate labour-market disruption has not yet appeared clearly in the data. Leaders should redesign apprenticeship before entry-level work disappears by accident.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2106187293501411755
