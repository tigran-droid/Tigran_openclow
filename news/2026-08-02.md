# Daily news — 2026-08-02

## Price AI work against business value
An automation consultant shared lessons from selling more than 100 systems.
His worked example priced a $5,500 build against $41,600 in annual labour value, then separated $400 monthly maintenance from new functionality.
The practical lesson is to establish the client baseline before quoting and measure the after-state after launch.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lg5TYWPSg6M

## Ten dollars buys a bespoke 3D world
Andrej Karpathy gave an AI model the opening of The Lord of the Rings, a one-million-token budget costing about $10, and two hours to work.
The model produced roughly 5,500 lines of code for an animated 3D scene.
The result was imperfect, and the model struggled to audit it because video perception and gameplay remain weak.
Source: https://x.com/karpathy/status/2083749667410727319

## Voice becomes a software interface
A demonstration used spoken instructions to create and modify an interactive 3D Iron Man suit.
The user changed colour, proportions and components through conversation.
The business implication is faster prototyping, but the ease of giving instructions does not remove the need for testing and ownership.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PaNIJ9l2NOE
