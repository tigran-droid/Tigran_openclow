# Daily news — 2026-10-21

## GPT-4 became the baseline
Ethan Mollick counts 180 models now better than GPT-4 at launch.
Nineteen can run on a modern smartphone.
The measured task horizon rose from four minutes in 2024 to 17 hours by April 2026 before the benchmark ran out.
Source: https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/30-months-on-the-exponential-in-charts

## OpenCode breach turns API keys into money
OpenCode said an attack affected 1.4% of console users and exposed stored model credentials.
Some users saw hundreds or thousands of dollars in charges.
The incident shows AI credentials now require financial-grade controls.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bw8nHS0_cl0

## Anthropic staff see a different future
Ninety-one percent of Anthropic staff expect AI to make a Nobel-level discovery by 2030, versus 39% of surveyed US adults.
Employee-directed agents were logging six times their human handlers' work hours by August.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nvyNQmOTKfY

## A restaurant operator proves the workflow
An independent franchisee says pricing analysis fell from five days to one or two hours.
Monthly cash reconciliation fell from three hours to five to ten minutes.
The figures are an OpenAI customer claim; the compare-correct-expand method is the practical lesson.
Source: https://openai.com/index/ted-taylor-restaurant

## Voice agents chase human timing
ElevenLabs says Realtime Max begins speaking in 0.57 seconds on average.
It claims more than 90 languages and over 17,000 voices.
Production value depends on interruption handling and completed outcomes, not demo quality.
Source: https://x.com/EugenioFierro3/status/2112582987451695580
