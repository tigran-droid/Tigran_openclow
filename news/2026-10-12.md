# Daily news — 2026-10-12

## AI mathematics hits a verification wall
OpenAI published 722 mathematical results, according to the AI Daily Brief.
Some mathematicians described individual results as career-level work.
The constraint is shifting from production to verification and comprehension.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GoTIt8aPG0Y

## The capability gap becomes a funnel
Andrej Karpathy estimated that roughly 6 billion people have barely encountered LLMs.
Around 20 million professionals may be seeing frontier systems transform complex technical work firsthand.
Only about 5,000 people have access to the strongest internal systems.
Source: https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046

## AI returns look stronger, but hiring barely moves
A survey cited by the AI Daily Brief found 35% reporting returns worth multiples of AI spend.
Another 17% reported positive returns below expectations.
Nearly half said AI had not altered hiring practices.
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GoTIt8aPG0Y

## Sudden product changes keep hurting enterprises
Ethan Mollick said leaders at large firms were still committed to GPTs shortly before they were sunsetted.
Enterprise planning cycles cannot match sudden laboratory product changes.
Portable context and workflow logic are the practical response.
Source: https://x.com/emollick/status/2109472254417318335

## Agent tooling moves from demos to delivery
A review of more than 100 Claude repositories highlighted specification, testing, security and integration tools.
The useful pattern is to solve one bottleneck and verify the finished workflow.
